Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does this app include the new 2026/2027 AIM Act A2L refrigerants?
A: Yes! Our question banks and diagnostic drills are fully updated with the latest EPA AIM Act phase-down rules, ASHRAE Standard 15/34 classifications, and safety handling requirements for mildly flammable A2L refrigerants like R-454B and R-32.
Q: How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone or iPad?
A: Open the app, navigate to the Profile / Settings tab, and tap "Restore StoreKit Purchases". As long as you are signed into the same Apple ID, your license will immediately unlock.
Q: Is this app expenseable to my employer or union?
A: Yes. Most HVAC contractors, unions, and mechanical service companies provide tool and certification allowances that cover professional study software. You can use your Apple App Store digital email receipt as proof of expense.
