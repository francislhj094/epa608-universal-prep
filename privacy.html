Privacy Policy

1. Overview & On-Device Data Architecture

EPA 608 HVAC Exam Prep 2027 ("we," "our," or "the App") is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. 100% of your exam answers, practice session scores, bookmarks, and diagnostic assessment results are stored locally on your device using Apple's secure sandboxed storage.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

3. StoreKit Purchases & Financial Transactions

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed directly through Apple's StoreKit 2 infrastructure. We never receive, store, or process your credit card numbers or financial account information. Apple provides us only with anonymous cryptographic receipts to confirm active entitlements.
